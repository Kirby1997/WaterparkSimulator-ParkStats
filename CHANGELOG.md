# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First version. Adds tabs to the tablet's Park Management page:

- Today: net result, visitors, satisfaction and the most urgent advice.
- Money: today's income and expenses by reason, balance, lifetime totals, loans, staff cost.
- Rides: attractions by kind with state, uses today, price against ideal price, cleanliness, durability and guests waiting.
- Guests: each need with its average and how many guests are running low, cash, complaints, reasons for leaving.
- History: visitors, net result and satisfaction for past days.
- Advice: a ranked list of what is holding the park back.
