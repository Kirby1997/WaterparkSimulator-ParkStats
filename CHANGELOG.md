# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First version. Adds tabs to the tablet's Park Management page:

- Today: net result, visitors, satisfaction, prestige and the most urgent things to do.
- Money: today's income and expenses by reason, income per visitor, earnings by attraction, balance, lifetime totals, loans, staff cost.
- Rides: attractions by kind with state, uses today, price against ideal price, cleanliness, durability and guests waiting.
- Guests: each need with its average, how many guests are under half and which attractions raise it, what satisfaction does to visitor numbers, complaints, reasons for leaving.
- History: visitors, net result and satisfaction for past days.
- Advice: a ranked list of what is holding the park back, including what the next star needs.
